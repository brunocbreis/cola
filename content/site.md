Page title, meta description, and the numbers behind the plan slider.

`scroll_ms` is how long a screen change takes. `?ms=180` in the URL overrides it for one
visit, so the feel can be dialled in without a rebuild; `?ms=0` is instant.

`motion: off` removes every desktop transition as well, and `?motion=off` does it for one
visit. Touch keeps its own momentum and snap either way.
